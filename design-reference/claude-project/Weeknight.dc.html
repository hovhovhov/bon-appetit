Weeknight
iPhone · 390 × 844
A full-screen recipe feed wired straight into a five-day dinner plan, a grocery budget and one consolidated list. Every card in Discover is two taps from becoming Thursday's dinner, and every addition moves the budget bar and the shopping list at the same moment.
Visual direction
Deep forest green carries authority and gives photography something to sit against; leaf green is reserved for the one action that matters on each screen. Warm cream replaces white throughout, so the app reads as a kitchen rather than a clinic. Bricolage Grotesque sets headlines with editorial weight; Open Runde handles every number and label.
Information architecture
Four tabs: Plan (home, the weekly command centre), Discover (the feed), Saved, You (preferences). Shopping list and recipe detail are pushed views. Account and app settings live behind the avatar, top right of Plan.
Core loop
Discover a recipe → add it to a day → the budget bar and list totals recalculate → the week completes → shop from one list. The plan is never a dead end: an empty day always offers a route back into the feed, and every scheduled meal can be swapped in place.
